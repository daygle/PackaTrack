PackaTrack keeps every parcel in one place, even when the tracking number changes mid-journey. It is built for the messy reality of shopping from AliExpress, Cainiao, Australia Post and iMile: shipments that get renumbered at the border, orders consolidated into a single box, and tracking split across several carriers.

RENUMBERING DETECTED FOR YOU
• PackaTrack notices when a parcel is given a new tracking number and links the two together, instead of leaving you with a dead number and no history
• A renumbered parcel keeps one continuous timeline, with every scan from every number

CONSOLIDATED SHIPMENTS
• Attach several tracking numbers to one parcel when a seller combines your orders into one physical shipment
• See a merged view of every scan, from every courier, in date order

MULTI-COURIER TIMELINES
• Cainiao, Australia Post, iMile, Aramex and Morning Global scans merged into a single chronological timeline
• Carrier detection from the tracking number, so you rarely have to pick one yourself

KNOW WHAT IS LATE
• Colour-coded parcels (green, yellow, orange, red) based on how long they have been in transit
• Set the day thresholds for each colour to suit your own patience

NOTIFICATIONS THAT MATTER
• Alerted when a parcel is delivered, when its tracking number changes, and when shipments are combined
• Background sync keeps timelines fresh without you opening the app
• Optional import of order emails from Gmail, read on your device, to add parcels you would otherwise type in by hand

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No PackaTrack servers: tracking data is fetched directly from the carriers
• Your history stays on your device, in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) with its key held in the Android Keystore
• Optional app lock with biometrics or your device PIN
• Passphrase-encrypted export and import if you want to move your history to a new phone

MADE FOR ANDROID
• Native Material 3 design with light and dark themes
• Android 7.0 or newer
• Australia Post tracking needs a free API key from Australia Post, added in Settings

PackaTrack has no ads, no analytics and no cloud account. Your parcels are not sold, shared or sent anywhere except the carriers you track with.
